ESP32-S31-Korvo-1
=================

:link_to_translation:`zh_CN:[中文]`

The ESP32-S31-Korvo-1 is a multimedia development board based on the ESP32-S31-WROOM-3 module for smart audio and HMI applications. It features a dual-microphone array, speaker output, a microSD card slot, and an onboard DVP camera; supports speech recognition and voice wake-up; and comes with a 4.3-inch LCD subboard (ESP32-S3-LCD-EV-Board-SUB3). This document describes revision V1.1.
